Doing this responsibly

Many people in the room handle other people's sensitive information for a living. The workshop treats that as the starting point, not an afterthought.

Keep private data out

We practice on sample and scrubbed material, and cover exactly what should never be pasted into a general AI tool, client files, health details, and financial records included.

A human checks everything

AI drafts, people decide. Anything headed to a client, a family, or a filing gets read by the professional responsible for it first.

Know the limits

Tools can be confidently wrong. We cover the quick checks that catch errors before they reach a client, a parent, or a senior.

Common questions

Do I need a paid AI account?

No. Everything we do can be done with free versions of tools like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. If you already pay for one, bring it.

Is my client or business data safe?

We’ll cover data privacy in the first 15 minutes. You’ll learn how to anonymize sensitive details and turn off training settings before you paste anything in.

What if I’m not “tech-savvy”?

You’re in the right room. If you can send an email, you have all the technical background you need.

Is there a catch or a sales pitch?

None. This is a free community session, no software upsells, no lead-gen traps.
