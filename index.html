Agenda

Most of the session is hands-on work at your table, not slides.

First, see what is possible

Real life transformations of small business operations, with a concrete example for each area.

  • Marketing

    a week of social posts and customer emails drafted in one sitting.

  • Finance

    invoices, receipts, and cash flow summaries prepared automatically.

  • Admin

    scheduling, intake forms, and follow-up handled without the paper chase.

  • Operations

    checklists and hand-offs that keep the whole team in sync.

Then, the workshop

  • Pain point identification

    name the task that eats your week.

  • Ideation and system possibilities

    map what AI could take off your plate.

  • Honing projects

    leave with one working project, built on your own laptop.

Tactics for working with AI

  • Prompting

    how to ask so you get usable results the first time.

  • Tool connectors

    connect AI to the tools you already use, like email, calendars, and spreadsheets.
