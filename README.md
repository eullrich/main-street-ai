# Main Street AI

Our town learning together.

A free, 2 hour, hands-on community AI workshop for small businesses and community organizations, proposed for a local library or community center.

For independent CPAs, lawyers, insurance brokers, business owners, in-home care providers, senior community centers, childcare centers, libraries, and youth sports organizers.

Hosted by Eric Ullrich. This is a concept page for a proposed first Community Day. Date and venue are being finalized, and the Chamber of Commerce is an invited partner to be confirmed.

Live site: https://eullrich.github.io/main-street-ai/
